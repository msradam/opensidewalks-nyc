NYC planimetric sidewalk polygons are often a ring around a whole block, so strip formulas need the interior ring and a rectangle axis is not a centerline.

2*area/perimeter with the outer ring only doubles the width; the minimum-rotated-rectangle axis of a ring runs through the buildings. Evidence: research_notes/evidence/repro_si/dem_and_planimetric.json, imagery_sample/ratings.csv.
