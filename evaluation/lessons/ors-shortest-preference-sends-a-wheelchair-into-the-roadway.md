OpenRouteService's wheelchair profile avoids the roadway through its "recommended" weighting, not through a block; with `preference: shortest` it follows street centrelines like a foot route.

On 362 Brownsville pairs, 71.0% of ORS wheelchair routes under "shortest" ran more than 10 m along a street centreline and their median length equalled the foot route's; under the recommended weighting 1.9% did (Brooklyn: 97.3% against 13.4%). Whether a route is found is the same under both. So a comparison of route shape must use ORS's own weighting, and the claim "ORS finds a route for 97% of pairs" includes routes in the roadway: city-wide, 13% (Brooklyn) to 73% (Staten Island) of its routes spend more than 10 m there by this graph's data, and a blind check over 2024 imagery agreed at 10 of 12 such places. Evidence: research_notes/compare/results/comparison.json, disagreements/score.json.
