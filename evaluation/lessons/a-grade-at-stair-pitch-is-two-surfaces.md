An edge that is not steps and reads 50% or steeper has its two ends on two surfaces; most such edges are not where OSM tags a structure.

2026-10-05, v0.3.6. v0.3.5 had 926 edges at 50% or more that were neither steps nor tagged as a structure. Only 318 join a deck height to a terrain height. 590 have terrain heights at both ends: a sidewalk on an overpass OSM does not tag as a bridge beside the cut under it, a retaining wall, a path next to a pit. So the rule cannot key on `ext:elevation_source`; it keys on the grade. `pipeline.utils.deck.grade` writes no incline at a staircase's pitch (0.5) or more off steps, or over 1.0 on steps, and the edge gets `ext:incline_unknown=yes`. The wheelchair profile reads that mark as steeper than any limit; an edge with no incline and no mark (a tunnel) was not measured and still passes. The node heights beside a marked edge may be wrong themselves (terrain under an untagged bridge), and nothing fixes those yet.
