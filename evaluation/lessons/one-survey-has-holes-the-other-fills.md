The 2017 NYC point cloud has no returns on the main spans over open water, so a second survey is needed there, and the two surveys check each other everywhere else.

On the Brooklyn Bridge the 2017 tiles stop at the towers; the 2014 USGS survey has the span (deck 41.5 to 44.5 m). Where both have data they agree to a few centimetres on the same node, which is what makes the 2014 survey a check on heights taken from 2017: 86% within 0.25 m, 91% within 1 m, and the large disagreements are places rebuilt between the two flights (Hudson Yards, Empire Outlets, the Bayonne Bridge, LaGuardia). Evidence: research_notes/next/structure/validate_v032_dev.json.

Note added 2026-10-03: those shares are from the dry run on the v0.3.2 graph. On the final v0.3.3 build they are 83.4% within 0.25 m and 91.6% within 1 m, median 0.05 m (evaluation/structure_incline/structure_validate_2014.json).
