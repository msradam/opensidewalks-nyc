A bridge test that snaps approximate landfall points to the nearest pedestrian node measures the anchors as much as the bridge: two of the five "unjoined" bridges of v0.3.2 were joined all along.

The Henry Hudson walkway runs south-west to north-east and its north landing is 230 m from the test's point; the Randall's Island Connector's points were under the RFK span 200 m to the west. Put each anchor on the walkway's own landing, found from the OSM ways, before reading the ratio. Evidence: research_notes/next/bridges/FINDINGS.md.
