Taking a wrong value out of one step can break a later step that leaned on it: the deck rules used the terrain's 0 m over water as their floor.

2026-10-05, v0.3.6. When terrain "no data" stopped being read as 0 m ([[terrain-no-data-is-written-as-zero]]), nodes over water reached `_structure_elevations` with no terrain height. `deck.label_surfaces` drops a surface within 1 m of the terrain as "the ground seen past the deck", so with no terrain the water's own LiDAR returns passed for a deck: three nodes on the RFK Bridge went from 47 m to -0.9 m in the first rehearsal, and 14 deck heights moved in all. The fix passes sea level (0.0) to the deck step for those nodes as a floor and does not write it as a height. Compare every deck height with the previous build after any change to terrain sampling (`evaluation/v0.3.6/code/compare_v035.py` groups the changes by cause).
