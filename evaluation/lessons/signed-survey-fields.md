The NYC DOT ramp survey signs its slopes by direction, so compare magnitudes and read the data dictionary first.

A test of cross_slope <= 2 passes every negative value, so it counts far more ramps than a test on the magnitude does. This project does not publish a share of ramps within a design limit: DOT says its measurements are not indicative of whether a ramp is compliant. The same dictionary shows the units (percent, inches) and that DWS_CONDITIONS is never empty, and the data shows four sentinel codes (555, 777, 888, 999) the dictionary does not define. The handling is in `METHODOLOGY.md`, "Units and signs" and "Sentinel value handling".
