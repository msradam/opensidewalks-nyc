The NYC DOT ramp survey signs its slopes by direction, so compare magnitudes and read the data dictionary first.

A test of cross_slope <= 2 passed every negative value and reported 82.6% where the magnitude test gives 66%. The same dictionary shows the units (percent, inches) and that DWS_CONDITIONS is never empty, and the data shows four sentinel codes (555, 777, 888, 999) the dictionary does not define. Evidence: research_notes/ramp_standards.md.
