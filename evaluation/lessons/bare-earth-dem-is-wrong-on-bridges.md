A bare-earth (and topobathymetric) terrain model gives a bridge, deck or pier the ground or river bed below it, so incline and elevation there describe something other than the path.

With the wheelchair profile's slope limits this closed the Brooklyn Bridge: the route to DUMBO ran 9.1 km against 4.2 km with the limits off. Mark edges OSM tags as bridge or tunnel and write no incline on them. Node elevations on structures are still wrong (149 nodes below -1 m). Evidence: research_notes/release/routing/city_build3/reach.json.

Note added 2026-10-03: those lengths are from v0.3.2. On the final v0.3.3 build, with deck heights on structures, the wheelchair route from the Brooklyn Bridge to DUMBO is 3,769 m with the limits on (evaluation/reachability/reach.json).
