The LAS bridge deck class (17) covers road and rail bridges but not a plaza over a road or a deck on a building, and the ground class turns up beside and under decks, so neither class can be trusted alone.

At Revson Plaza (Columbia, over Amsterdam Avenue) the deck is unclassified, dense and flat at 48.6 m, while a few class 2 returns at 42.8 m come from the avenue below. Taking the classified surface put the plaza at street level. Under the elevated subway lines the opposite happened: the track structure is flat and dense, and once one station entrance mapped without steps let the deck claim a sidewalk node, it spread for blocks. The rule that holds: a node OSM puts on a structure prefers a solid surface above the terrain; a node OSM does not put on a structure stays at terrain height wherever the survey classified ground there, whatever hangs above it.
