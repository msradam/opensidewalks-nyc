A curb ramp run may be 1:12 (8.33%); 5% is the limit for a walkway, a counter slope and a blended transition.

2010 ADA Standards 406.1 sends curb ramps to 405.2 (1:12) and 405.3 (1:48 cross slope); PROWAG R304.2.1 and R304.2.2 say the same. Before publishing any share of ramps that meets a standard, quote the section, and prefer the owner agency's own determination (NYC DOT publishes per-ramp checks).
