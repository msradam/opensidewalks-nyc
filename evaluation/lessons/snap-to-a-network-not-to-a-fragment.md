The nearest passable edge to a point is sometimes a stub joined to nothing, and a search that starts there reports "no route" for a trip that begins three metres from the sidewalk network.

OpenRouteService never starts on such a stub: GraphHopper drops networks under 200 edges when it builds its graph. This graph's search did, and failed 10 of 420 Brownsville trips that ORS routed on the very same edges (arm C). With the snap taking the nearest edge of a network of 200 edges or more when it is at most 25 m further away, all 420 route, and the city-wide shares rose by 3 to 8 points (Brooklyn 87.2% to 91.2%, Manhattan 55.7% to 64.0%). A point whose only nearby edges are an island keeps its "no route". Evidence: compare/graph.py `snap_edge`, routes/ours_nearest_edge_any_fragment.pkl against routes/ours.pkl.
