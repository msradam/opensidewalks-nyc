# Routes checked by hand over orthoimagery

2026-10-03, final build (commit `2442db2`). The images were drawn over NY State "Latest" orthoimagery (about 30 cm here), which the pipeline does not use. The images are not published, because no licence statement for that imagery was found; their file names are kept below for reference. One rater. The check shows whether a route lies on sidewalks, crossings, paths and bridge decks; it cannot see a kerb ramp.

Colours: cyan sidewalk, yellow crossing, green footway (bridge decks and park paths are footways), magenta steps, red street centreline. No wheelchair route below has a magenta or red segment.
